# Diagnosis: report 2026-10-04-6c0f163, k12_hard × coppa × rules+model

Read from the regression report only (`evidence/reports/2026-10-04-6c0f163/03_k12_hard_model_python.diagnostics.json`
and `summary.md`). No sealed set was opened. Proposals are for the edshield
repository; nothing here changes the bench.

## The number

334 of 1,433 gold identifiers survive whole in the output (identifier recall
0.767, interval 0.739 to 0.791); 24 more survive partially. edshield's own
count is 319 "got through"; the 15 extra are spans a flag touched without
removing the whole string. Over-redaction is low: 27 of 20,839
non-identifier tokens removed (word fpr 0.13%), almost all chat shorthand
(`tmrw`, `rn`, `r`, `its`) swept up next to a phone number or e-mail.

## Where the misses are, and what they look like

| label | n | whole residuals | pattern in the residual examples | tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AGE | 205 | 96 | bare numbers (`11`, `7`) and chat forms (`7m`, `11m`, `14m`) with no cue word; 7 of 58 examples were touched by an AGE flag and still survive, which is the bench's conservative match on a two-character number that also occurs elsewhere in the document | rules; see caveat |
| LOCATION | 122 | 83 | town names with no cue: lowercase (`woodtown`, `kennethport`) and capitalised one- or two-word (`West Kelly`, `New Michaelshire`). 25 more were caught only as NAME_STUDENT (`Lake Natalie`, `port samantha`): the model takes the person-name half and the direction word survives, hence 18 of the 24 partial residuals | rules, then spans |
| SCHOOL | 116 | 74 | lowercase `<surname> middle school` / `<surname> elementary`, and a bare surname used as the school name (`Reed`, `Richards`); the SCHOOL rule wants capitalised words | rules |
| DATE | 71 | 38 | every one is the spoken form `16th of February`; the DATE rule has no `<ordinal> of <month>` shape | rules |
| STREET_ADDRESS | 34 | 34 | street name without a house number (`Mayer Drive`, `Burke Street`); the rule requires a leading number | rules |
| NAME_STUDENT | 351 | 9 | lowercase or all-caps single names (`jack`, `MEADOWS`, `wayne`) the model misses when there is no casing signal | model (later) |

Everything else is at or near 100%: EMAIL, USERNAME, ID_NUM, PHONE_NUM,
NAME_RELATED. Label confusion worth knowing but not a leak under coppa
(every label is masked): PHONE_NUM is flagged as ID_NUM 72 times out of 75
(run-together ten-digit numbers), NAME_RELATED as NAME_STUDENT 200 of 240.
The masks differ (`[ID_NUM]` for a phone, `[CHILD]` for a parent), which
matters for the `replace` method and for readers, not for residuals.

## Proposed changes, cheapest first (per docs/LOOP.md)

1. **DATE rule: spoken ordinals.** Add `<1-31><st|nd|rd|th> of <Month>` and
   `<Month> <1-31><st|nd|rd|th>` without a year. Expected: most of the 38.
2. **STREET_ADDRESS rule: name + suffix without a house number**, when the
   suffix is one of the unambiguous street words (Drive, Street, Avenue,
   Lane, Court, Road, Boulevard…) and the preceding token is capitalised or
   the phrase follows `on`/`at`/`near`. Expected: most of the 34, at some
   precision cost on PIILO (watch the word fpr target there).
3. **SCHOOL rule: case-insensitive school kinds** (`middle school`,
   `elementary`, `high school`, `academy`) with one or two preceding tokens
   of any case when the phrase follows `at`/`to`/`go to`/`from`. Expected:
   the 20 lowercase forms; the bare-surname schools (`Reed`) need context the
   rules do not have.
4. **AGE rule: chat forms.** `<n>m`, `<n>f`, `<n> yo`, bare `<n>` right after
   `im`/`i'm`/`turning` is already covered; add the `<n>m`/`<n>f` suffix and
   `age <n>` inside a profile-like line. Expected: the 10 or so suffix forms;
   the bare numbers remain.
5. **LOCATION spans: extend a name hit over a leading direction word.** When
   the model flags `Natalie` in `Lake Natalie` / `port samantha`, a span rule
   that absorbs a preceding `North|South|East|West|New|Lake|Port|Fort|Mount`
   turns 18 partial residuals into full removals. Towns with no name inside
   (`woodtown`) need the model or a gazetteer, which is a later tier.
6. **Model (last).** Lowercase and all-caps names, and lowercase towns and
   schools, are the retrain case edshield's own notes already describe
   (child-style synthetic text, worded differently from the hard set).

Do not write any of these against the k12_hard templates. The hard set is
dev-visible and the regression floor; a rule should be stated for the
general child register and then checked here.

## Measurement caveats raised by this report

- **Short numeric identifiers.** A gold `11` counts as a residual whenever
  `11` occurs anywhere else in the output as a whole word. For AGE this
  overstates residuals somewhat (7 of 58 examples). Keep the rule (the judge
  cannot know which `11` is the age) and read AGE recall as a lower bound.
- **Spoken e-mails.** `patriciagarcia at gmail dot com` is flagged as EMAIL,
  yet `gmail` is reported as a partial residual: the mask covers the whole
  phrase and the token appears elsewhere in the document. Reported, not
  gated; harmless.
- **Parity.** The INT8 export agreed with the PyTorch model on every PIILO
  identifier (agreement 1.0000). k12_hard parity was not run; add it to the
  baseline if a browser-only regression is ever suspected.

## What would move the acceptance verdict

Acceptance needs identifier recall ≥ 0.995 with the interval clearing it and
every type ≥ 0.98. Items 1 to 5 together address about 190 of the 334 whole
residuals on this set (the shapes with a rule-expressible pattern); the
remaining bare towns, bare schools, bare ages and cased-out names are the
model's. Expect the rules tier to reach roughly 0.90 identifier recall on
k12_hard and the model tier to be needed for 0.995.
