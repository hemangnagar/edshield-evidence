# Agent-assisted diagnosis of the genre-shift results: findings

Every number below is read from a file on this branch, named in brackets. Nothing is merged; the two patches are proposals for a human reviewer, measured by the unchanged bench scorer.

**What share of misses is rule-fixable.** An Opus 5.5 batch labelled every missed identifier in both views with a closed cause taxonomy (501 rows, 98% primary-cause agreement when the taxonomy order was reversed) [`results/diagnosis/summary.md`]. On the sealed deterministic view, 80.0% of the 275 misses were classed RULE or GAZETTEER and 17.5% MODEL; on the LLM-planted view, 84.1% of 226 misses were RULE and 12.4% MODEL [`results/diagnosis/causes.jsonl`]. One pattern dominates: a student addressed by an initial ("Well done, V.") is caught 1.2% of the time (2 of 168) with the sealed templates and 0 of 164 with LLM phrasing [`experiments/genre_shift/results/metrics.json`, `results/llm_view/llm/metrics.json`]. Name-word collisions are the largest MODEL-class cause (43 and 18 rows).

**What the second view showed about template dependence.** Re-planting the same slots with Sonnet 5.5 wording, same name forms and categories, 724 of 738 dialogues validated [`data/planted_llm/plant_llm_log.json`]. Pooled identifier recall barely moves: 90.3% [89.2, 91.4] with the templates, 91.8% [90.8, 92.8] with LLM phrasing. CoMTA, the only real-student set, does move: 85.3% [82.3, 87.9] against 91.7% [89.2, 93.6], intervals disjoint, driven by name-word collisions (65.5% to 82.4%) [`results/view_comparison.md`]. The sealed templates are harsher on collision names than an LLM's phrasing; the initial-form failure is identical in both.

**What the bounded loop recovered, and at what cost.** One Managed Agents session (Opus 5.5, $30 cap, $3.77 spent, stop reason `end_turn`) ran two rounds and stopped by its own projection before a third [`proposals/PROPOSAL.md`, `runs/20261015T142426Z-agent/SESSION.json`]. Round 1 propagates a detected name to its standalone initial; round 2 adds "High" and "Prep" as school suffixes. Both touch only `edshield/rules.py` and apply cleanly to a568e73 [`proposals/DRIVER_CHECK.md`]. Under the INT8 ONNX runtime, deterministic-view identifier recall goes from 89.97% (round 0, unmodified checkout, within 0.35 pp of the sealed GPU baseline) to 95.32%, and the LLM view from 91.83% to 97.07%; control over-redaction is unchanged on both datasets at 0.503 and 15.102 flags per 1,000 tokens [`results/candidate/round_0/summary.json`, `results/candidate/round_2/summary.json`]. Initial forms rise from 1.2% to 78.6% [`results/candidate/round_2/deterministic/metrics.json`]. _Driver re-score: pending._

**What remains model work.** After both patches, 133 deterministic misses remain: 58 name-word collisions (CoMTA collision recall still 62.1%), 40 "elsewhere" cases including 33 one-letter residuals the scorer's conservative matching counts against masked initials, 22 nickname forms, 7 lowercase names and 4 typos [`results/candidate/round_2/deterministic/metrics.json`, `proposals/PROPOSAL.md`]. Rules cannot separate "Summer" or "Grace" the student from the words without context; that, and casing robustness, is the model claim.

Spend for the whole branch: $11.09 of $71 at list price [`runs/20261015T144955Z-cost/COST.md`].
